How do I close a boundary to measure area?

Tap at least 3 corners on the satellite map, then tap back on the glowing green start corner (or tap "Close boundary to measure area").

How do I adjust or remove a corner?

After finishing your boundary, tap any white corner dot on the map to bring up the precision crosshair and drag handle. Tap Delete on the bottom bar to remove an extra corner, or tap Undo to revert.

How do I restore AcreQuote Pro on a new device?

Open Business & Settings (gear icon) and tap Restore purchases while signed into the same Apple Account.
