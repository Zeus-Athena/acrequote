How do I close a boundary to measure area?

Tap at least 3 corners on the satellite map, then tap back on the glowing green start corner — or tap Close loop on the bottom bar. You can also tap Walk to measure a boundary outdoors with GPS.

How do I adjust, split, or remove a corner?

After finishing your boundary, tap any white corner dot on the map to bring up the precision crosshair and offset drag handle, or drag any small white midpoint dot on an edge to split that side. Tap Delete on the bottom bar to remove an extra corner, or tap Undo to revert.

How do I restore AcreQuote Pro on a new device?

Open the Settings tab and tap Restore purchases while signed into the same Apple Account.
