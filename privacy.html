Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

AcreQuote is designed to operate completely on your iPhone and iPad without requiring an account or storing your business data on developer-operated servers.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect, sell, rent, or track your personal data, project measurements, client names, addresses, signatures, or generated PDF estimates. All project records and business settings remain stored locally on your device.

2. Location & Apple Maps

If you choose to tap the Locate button, AcreQuote requests temporary "When In Use" location access solely to center the map on your current position. Satellite imagery, address autocomplete, and map snapshots are provided directly by Apple MapKit in accordance with Apple's privacy policy.

3. Photos

If you select a company logo using the system photo picker, only the single image you select is stored locally on your device for rendering onto your PDF estimates.

4. In-App Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed exclusively by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. AcreQuote does not have access to your payment card or billing details.

5. Contact

If you have privacy questions, please reach out via our Support page.
